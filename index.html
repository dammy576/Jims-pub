About Jim's

Pull up a stool. Stay a while.

Jim's is the kind of place where the bartender remembers your order and the regulars save you a seat. Worn wood, warm light, a good jukebox, and no pretense — just a neighborhood pub doing simple things well.

The heart of the room is our wood-fired oven. Every pie gets a blistered, chewy crust and a kiss of smoke in about ninety seconds flat. Pair it with a cold pint and you've got the best night of your week.
